Authenticated caller (MDC actor)customer-service  ← internal i2e1 service, NOT a device. 100% of ~3.6k events carry this single actor.
TransitionT7 PAUSED→PENDING_DEACTIVATION (2,381) or T6 ACTIVE→PENDING_DEACTIVATION (1,241)
Key point for tech: source_surface=CUSTOMER_APP is a caller-supplied body field on the /events/… ingress (service-to-service, gated only by a service token). It is not derived from an authenticated device session, so the DB label alone does not prove a real app tap.
