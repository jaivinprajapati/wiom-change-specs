Are our records right about the devices our live customers are using?

A check across every paying customer, and what we are fixing.

8 August 2026

Every live customer has a device in their home. Our systems should show that device as deployed and working. We checked whether they do.

Almost all are right. A small share are not, and some of those are costing us money.

Two rows are not device states. No device on record — we hold no device against this customer at all. More than one device — two records exist for one customer, usually an old swap left open.

Why this matters

A wrong record has real cost.

  • A device marked lost or written off is treated as gone. Its value is already off the books.
  • Partners can be charged for devices that were never actually lost.
  • Support and field teams work off the wrong picture.
  • We cannot count our own assets accurately.

Step 4 — What we are doing now

  1. Restore the lost and written-off devices
    These are the unambiguous ones. The customer is paying and connected, so the device is neither lost nor gone.
    743devices restored to deployed
  2. Match how the system already behaves
    Our platform already makes this same correction automatically when a connection is reactivated. We reproduce exactly that, nothing more.
  3. Hold the rest
    Pickups, missing records and duplicates each need a different decision. We return to them once the cause is clear.
    1,206cases on hold
One point needs a decision. 253 of the 256 written-off devices were written off deliberately by the July finance audit. Restoring them reverses that decision. Finance should confirm before we proceed.
