Are our records right about the devices our live customers are using?

A check across every paying customer, and what we are fixing.

8 August 2026

Every live customer has a device in their home. Our systems should show that device as deployed and working. We checked whether they do.

Almost all are right. We examined every one that was not, and are fixing what is genuinely wrong.

Where the customer list came from. We did not ask the device system who its customers are. We started from the recharge and connection records — the independent evidence that someone is paying and online right now — and only then asked the device system what it holds against them. A system cannot be checked against its own answer.

Step 4 — Customers do return devices while still paying

Five of those states mean the device has come back to us — CUSTOMER_RECOVERY_PENDING, IDLE, RETRIEVAL_PENDING, RETURNED and CUSTODIED. That is not always an error. Customers sometimes hand a device back while their plan is still running. So for each of these 830 customers we traced the device history, to see whether it genuinely left the customer or whether the record was simply created that way.

Most of the 45 RETRIEVAL_PENDING records were created on a single day, 16 June. That points to one cause, not 45 separate problems.

One point needs a decision. 253 of the 256 written-off devices were written off deliberately by the July finance audit. Restoring them reverses that decision. Finance should confirm before we proceed.

Why this matters

A wrong record has real cost.

  • A device marked lost or written off is treated as gone. Its value is already off the books.
  • Partners can be charged for devices that were never actually lost.
  • Support and field teams work off the wrong picture.
  • We cannot count our own assets accurately.

Open questions

  • What put 45 devices into RETRIEVAL_PENDING on 16 June with no history behind it?
  • Should 233 customers who returned their device still be on a paid plan?
  • Should the 994 customers on not-yet-onboarded partners be in scope at all?
