Old-App Wallet Ledger Visibility Cut-off for Migrated Partners

A migrated partner sees two wallet ledgers in the new CSP app: the new wallet ledger and their old-app wallet history. Old-app entries dated after the partner's migration cut-over must no longer be shown, so the old ledger ends exactly where the new one begins — old closing balance = new opening balance. Display-only: no balance, transaction, or money movement changes anywhere.

Author: Jaivin Prajapati (PM)  ·  Reviewer: Engineering  ·  Tier: Light (A3/A4/A7/B1–B4 skipped; A5 rows are self-contained)

Quick Check

7-line triage. Filled after the body was completed.
What is changing?
A migrated partner's old-app wallet history now ends at their migration cut-over; post-cut-over old-app entries are no longer displayed.
What triggered it?
Migrated-wallet reconciliation: partners see the same post-migration activity in both ledgers, so the two ledgers don't reconcile on screen and money appears double-counted.
Blast radius
Old-app wallet history display for migrated partners; old-ledger entry point for partners without old history. New wallet ledger is read-only reference. Actors: migrated CSP partners, finance-ops/support.
Reversible?
Yes — the visibility rule can be removed to restore today's full display; no data is changed.
Money / PII / migration?
Money: display-only — no balance, amount, or movement changes (guarded in A6/B5). PII: No. Migration: No.
OS impact / sign-off?
None — Payment & Settlement OS behaviour, contracts and data semantics unchanged (declared No in A8 with reason). No sign-offs required.
Tier
Light — contained display-logic change, no state machine, no new parameters. (Lead PM: Jaivin Prajapati)

A2. Why This Change WHAT

QuestionAnswer
What's wrong with the current behaviour?A migrated partner's old-app wallet history is shown in full, including entries dated after their migration. Every post-migration transaction therefore appears in both ledgers, and the old ledger's closing balance no longer matches the new ledger's opening balance — the two ledgers visibly fail to reconcile.
Who experiences it?Migrated CSP partners reviewing their wallet history (money appears double-counted), and finance-ops/support who field the resulting confusion and reconciliation disputes.
Cost of leaving it as-is?Recurring partner disputes and support load on every migrated partner who scrolls their history; erosion of trust in the new wallet at exactly the moment partners are being moved onto it.

A5. The Delta WHAT

What changesBeforeAfterWhy
1 · Old-app history visibility for a migrated partnerThe partner's entire old-app wallet history is shown, including entries dated after their migration.Old-app entries dated after the partner's cut-over are not shown. The visible old history ends at the cut-over, so the last visible old-app balance equals the new ledger's opening balance.Post-cut-over activity already lives in the new ledger; showing it twice breaks on-screen reconciliation.
2 · Cut-over definitionNo cut-over concept exists for display purposes.A partner's cut-over is the moment of their first entry in the new wallet ledger — any entry kind, not only the migration opening credit. Timestamps are compared in UTC.Uniform for every partner, including the known case whose new ledger starts with a payout rather than an opening credit. The first new-ledger entry is, by definition, where the new ledger takes over.
3 · Old-ledger entry point for partners without a cut-overThe old-ledger entry point can be presented regardless of whether the partner has any old-app history.A partner with no new-ledger entry yet (in practice only a newly onboarded partner, who has no old-app history) is not shown the old-ledger entry point at all.No old history exists to display; offering an empty ledger is meaningless and confusing. Migrated partners always have at least one new-ledger entry, so they are unaffected.

A6. Must-Not-Change — Regression Guard GUARD

Adjacent behaviour that must not changeWhy it's in the blast radius
1 · Old-app entries up to and including the cut-over are shown exactly as today — same entries, same order, same values.The truncation rule must not over-truncate or reorder; only strictly-after-cut-over entries disappear.
2 · The new wallet ledger — content, balances, ordering, display — is unchanged.The new ledger is read to derive the cut-over; reading it must not alter anything about it.
3 · No balance, transaction amount, or money movement changes anywhere, in either system.The change is adjacent to partner money; it must remain display-only.
4 · The old-app system's data is never modified — the change only filters what is displayed.The old ledger lives in an external system; this change must remain read-only towards it.

B5. Acceptance Criteria — Verification Layer HOW

Business logic

LOGIC_01 (business logic · happy): Old history ends at the cut-over — A5-1
Given a migrated partner whose old-app history has entries both before and after their cut-over
When they view their old-app wallet history in the new app
Then no entry dated after the cut-over is shown
And the last visible old-app entry's balance equals the new ledger's opening balance
LOGIC_02 (business logic · rule): Cut-over is the first new-ledger entry of any kind — A5-2
Given a migrated partner whose first new-ledger entry is not a migration opening credit (e.g. it is a payout)
When their cut-over is determined
Then it is the moment of that first entry, the same rule as for every other partner
And their old-app history is truncated at that moment
LOGIC_03 (business logic · negative): No cut-over → no old ledger offered — A5-3
Given a partner with no entry yet in the new wallet ledger (a newly onboarded partner)
When they use the wallet area of the app
Then the old-ledger entry point is not shown to them at all

Computation & calculation

COMPUTE_01 (computation · boundary): The boundary entry stays visible
Given an old-app entry whose timestamp is exactly equal to the partner's cut-over
When the old-app history is displayed
Then that entry remains visible — only entries strictly after the cut-over are hidden
And the entry that established the closing balance is therefore never hidden
COMPUTE_02 (computation · boundary): Comparison is in UTC, regardless of display timezone
Given an old-app entry posted near midnight IST on the cut-over day
When it is compared against the cut-over
Then the comparison uses the entry's UTC instant, not its displayed local time
And no entry is wrongly hidden or shown because of the IST offset

Invariants

INVARIANT_01: Old closing equals new opening
Given any migrated partner
Then the last visible old-app balance always equals the new ledger's opening balance
And this holds for every migrated partner
INVARIANT_02: Display-only — no stored money value ever changes
Given any partner at any moment after this change ships
Then no balance, transaction amount, or ledger entry content differs from before the change, in either system
And no stored data anywhere is created, modified, or deleted by this change

Use & edge cases

EDGE_01 (edge): Browsing across the cut-over stays seamless
Given a migrated partner browsing their old-app history page by page
When their browsing reaches the cut-over point
Then the history simply ends at the cut-over with no broken, stuck, or odd paging behaviour
EDGE_02 (edge): Partner with no old-app history
Given a partner whose old-app system returns no history at all
When they use the wallet area
Then behaviour is unchanged from today — there is nothing to truncate and nothing new appears

Regression (from A6)

REGRESSION_01 (guard · A6-1): Pre-cut-over history identical
Given a migrated partner's old-app entries dated up to and including the cut-over
When the history is displayed after this change
Then those entries appear exactly as before — same entries, same order, same values
REGRESSION_02 (guard · A6-2): New wallet ledger untouched
Given any partner viewing their new wallet ledger
When this change is live
Then the new ledger's content, balances, ordering and display are exactly as before the change
REGRESSION_03 (guard · A6-3): No money moved
Given all partner balances and transactions in both systems
When this change ships
Then every balance and amount is unchanged — the change is provably display-only
REGRESSION_04 (guard · A6-4): Old-app system never written
Given the old-app wallet system
When this change operates, for any partner and any volume of history
Then no data in the old-app system is created, modified, or deleted by the new app
